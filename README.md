One image that sums up the world's news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026** —
a bright broadsheet front page, drawn with Python PIL, with every headline
legible.

## The day

- **GULF WAR WIDENS** — Iran strikes U.S. bases in Kuwait (Ali Al Salem)
  and Bahrain after American jets hit a missile site on Qeshm Island.
  A schematic Gulf map marks every strike.
- **KERCH BRIDGE FALLS — AGAIN** — a second-day Ukrainian strike drops
  part of the road span into the strait.
- **XI MEETS PUTIN IN SHENZHEN** — three-way talks with Trump set
  for Thursday.
- **DEMOCRATS TAKE THE SENATE** — Peltola wins Alaska; the party now
  holds 56 seats.
- Also: Ebola passes 11,000 cases in Congo; the U.S. loses its
  measles-free status; a Russian plane crash kills 19; Cyclone Xavier
  hits the Cook Islands.

## How it works

`draw.py` renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL + DejaVu fonts).
`main.ts` only copies that pre-rendered image to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
— no subprocess, no network, deterministic byte-for-byte output.
